# Adventure OS

Plan the logistics. Preserve the adventure.

First expedition: Guatemala & Baja, April 17–May 20, 2027. ILS 25,000 editable target. The 34 calendar days include arrival and departure. International flights need user-entered booking details. Segment dates are illustrative, not reservations. Mobula sightings are never guaranteed.

## Vertical slices

1. Persistent foundation: local SQLite, multi-expedition workspace, Hebrew/English shell, dashboard, task/destination/segment CRUD.
2. Operational planning: booking workflow with separate payment state, transport records, budget calculations, gear inventory and expedition packing.
3. Decision support: task dependencies, date-change impact review, research options, cloned itinerary scenarios, additive budget scenarios, actual/planned dive and training logs.
4. Daily use: mobile travel mode, emergency contacts, JSON export/restore, regression tests and deployment guidance.

The implementation delivers these slices as a local first MVP. Facts and plans remain distinct. Entries that require official or operator verification remain unresolved until the user checks authoritative sources. Fitness, altitude awareness and diving experience are independent considerations; no medical or activity clearance is generated.
